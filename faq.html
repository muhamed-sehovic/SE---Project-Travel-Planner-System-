Account & Access
Is Travel Planner free to use?
Yes — Travel Planner is completely free. Create an account, build unlimited trips, and use all core features at no cost. No credit card is required to sign up.
How do I create an account?
Click Login / Register in the navigation bar, then select Register. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a password. Once submitted you're immediately logged in and ready to plan.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
On the login page, click Forgot password? and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a reset link — follow it to set a new password. If no email arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.
Can I change my email address or username?
Yes. Head to your Profile Settings page (click your name in the navigation bar once logged in) and update your email, name, or password there.
Can I use Travel Planner on my phone?
Yes. The site is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser. There is no separate app to install — just open the site, log in, and everything adapts to your screen size.
Trips & Itineraries
How do I create a new trip?
Go to My Trips and click the + New Trip button. Enter a trip name, start date, end date, and an optional total budget. Once saved, you can open the trip to add destinations and itinerary activities.
How many trips can I create?
There is no limit. Create as many trips as you need — past, present, and future.
How do I add activities to my itinerary?
Open a trip card and click Open. In the trip detail view, switch to the Itinerary tab and click + Add Activity. Fill in the activity title, date, time, and an optional description. Activities are sorted automatically by date and time.
Can I add multiple destinations to one trip?
Yes. Inside the trip detail view, use the Destinations tab to search for and add as many destinations as you like. Each destination can include notes and will appear as a pin on the interactive map.
How do I delete a trip?
On the My Trips page, click the red trash icon on any trip card. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is permanently deleted. This also removes all itinerary items, expenses, and destinations linked to that trip.
What do the trip statuses mean?
Statuses are calculated automatically from your trip dates: Ongoing means today falls between the start and end date; Planned means the trip starts in the future; Completed means the end date has passed. You don't need to set these manually.
Budget & Expenses
How does budget tracking work?
When creating or editing a trip, you can set a total budget in USD. Inside the trip detail, log individual expenses under the Expenses tab — each with an amount, category, and optional note. The budget bar updates in real time to show how much you've spent and how much remains.
What expense categories are available?
Available categories are: Transport, Accommodation, Food, Activities, Flights, and Other. Each category has a distinct colour and emoji for quick scanning.
What happens when I go over budget?
The budget bar turns red and an "Over budget" warning appears with the amount exceeded. You won't be blocked from adding more expenses — the tracker is informational, not a hard limit.
Can I edit or delete an expense after saving it?
Yes. In the Expenses tab of any trip, each expense row has an edit (pencil) and delete (trash) icon. Changes update the budget totals instantly.
Sharing & Privacy
Can I share my trip with friends or family?
Yes. Open a trip and use the Share option to generate a read-only link. Anyone with the link can view your itinerary and destinations — but cannot edit anything, and your expense amounts stay private.
Is my personal data safe?
Your data is stored securely and never sold to third parties. Passwords are hashed before storage. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data we collect and how it's used.
Who can see my trips?
Only you can see your trips when logged in. Trips are private by default. They become visible to others only if you explicitly generate and share a share link.
How do I delete my account?
To request account deletion, please contact us. We'll permanently remove your account and all associated data within 7 business days.
Technical
Which browsers are supported?
Travel Planner works in all modern browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience keep your browser up to date. Internet Explorer is not supported.
The map isn't loading on my trip. What should I do?
The interactive map requires destinations with saved latitude/longitude coordinates. If you added a destination without coordinates, the map will show a placeholder message. Try refreshing the page, or contact support if the issue persists.
I get logged out unexpectedly. Why?
Your session token expires after a period of inactivity for security reasons. Simply log in again. If this happens very frequently, check that your browser is not set to clear cookies or local storage on close.
Something isn't working as expected. Where do I report it?
Please use the Contact Us page to describe the issue. Include your browser, the page you were on, and steps to reproduce — it helps us fix things faster.
