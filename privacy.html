1 Overview

Travel Planner ("we", "us") is an academic project built at the International Burch University (IBU) for the IT 309 Software Engineering course. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Service, how we use it, and what choices you have.

By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of data as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2 Data We Collect

CategoryDataWhen collected
AccountFirst name, last name, email address, hashed passwordAt registration
Trip dataTrip names, dates, budgets, destinations, itinerary activities, expenses, notesWhen you create or edit content
UsagePages visited, features used (server logs only — no third-party analytics)During each session
TechnicalIP address, browser type, device typeAutomatically on each request

We do not collect payment information, location data (beyond destination coordinates you manually enter), or data from social networks.

3 How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected solely to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide the trip planning, itinerary, and budget features
  • Send password-reset emails when you request them
  • Diagnose and fix technical errors
  • Improve the Service as part of the academic project

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

4 Storage & Security

Your data is stored on a server operated by the IBU development team. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Password hashing using a secure algorithm (plain-text passwords are never stored)
  • HTTPS encryption for data in transit
  • Authentication tokens that expire after a period of inactivity

As this is an academic project, we cannot guarantee enterprise-grade security. We recommend not storing highly sensitive information in your trip notes.

5 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party. Data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your consent — when you generate a share link, your trip itinerary (excluding expenses) becomes visible to anyone who has the link.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or a valid legal process.
  • Academic assessment — anonymised, aggregated usage patterns may be reviewed by IBU course supervisors.

6 Cookies & Local Storage

Travel Planner does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We use your browser's localStorage to store:

  • Your authentication token (to keep you logged in across page loads)
  • Basic user information (name, email) for display purposes

This data is stored only on your device and is cleared when you log out. You can also clear it manually through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your name, email, or password in Profile Settings at any time
  • Deletion — request full deletion of your account and all associated data via Contact Us
  • Portability — request an export of your trip data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 7 business days.

8 Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

10 Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please use our Contact Us page.
